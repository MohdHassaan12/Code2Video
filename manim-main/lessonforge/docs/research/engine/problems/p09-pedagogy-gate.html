Pedagogy compiles unverified

The compiler thesis has a stated boundary, and this is it: “a pedagogically weak learning plan compiles into a pedagogically weak video, flawlessly.” No gate anywhere scores whether the explanation teaches.
problems / 09
C · models own content — and content is ungated T3 open / frontier Maturity: L0 — no pedagogy gate exists
⚙️ Mechanics today
  • Pedagogy is planned once, up front: ce/planner_agent.py produces a PARTS plan (generate_pedagogical_plan:81), sizes scenes (determine_scene_count:56, floor MIN_LESSON_SCENES=4), and sets pace (narration_wpm:42).
  • Everything downstream verifies form: storyboard gates check truncation/shape/language; the probe checks geometry; Vision QA checks pixels. The teaching itself passes through ungated — the repo states the limitation verbatim chapter-4-architecture.md:371-382.
  • Pedagogical constants are load-bearing and silent: the 90-wpm early-primary pace was mis-applied to Grades 10–12 by a substring bug ("grade 1" in "grade 11", ce/planner_agent.py:42-53) — a pedagogy defect that no gate could even have named.
🕳️ The gap — why this is T3

Every other gate in this system rests on a computable ground truth: coordinates, schemas, scripts. Pedagogy’s ground truth — did a learner understand — is unobservable at build time and expensive to observe at all. Proxies exist (rubrics, LLM judges, expert review) but none is validated for generated Hinglish STEM explainers, and a bad proxy is worse than none: it would tune the pipeline toward plausible-sounding teaching, the precise failure the architecture was built to escape. This is the hardest problem on the site, and the top of the funnel: a perfect pipeline below a weak plan ships polished mediocrity at scale.

📎 Evidence
  • The shipped catalogue’s worst content failure was pedagogical, not technical: the 23.7s Dalton video that “never actually states the law” pipeline-roadmap.md:708 — caught by a human, fixed by a scene-count floor, generalized by nothing.
  • The scene floor itself (MIN_LESSON_SCENES=4) is the only pedagogy “gate” in the codebase — a structural proxy installed after that incident pipeline-roadmap.md:91-94.
  • Stage B’s grade note concedes it: “still no full review gate, so not L2.5” pipeline-roadmap.md:72.
📚 Prior art citation-check before external use

Multimedia-learning research (Mayer: coherence, signaling, segmenting, modality, redundancy) supplies checkable principles — several are structural enough to lint (redundant on-screen text vs. narration; unsignaled topic shifts). Intelligent-tutoring-systems literature has decades of evaluation methodology, almost all of it requiring learners. LLM-judge scoring of explanations is young and unvalidated for pedagogy; small-N comprehension studies (quiz-after-video) are the honest instrument and the expensive one.

🪜 Solution sketch — cheap → deep
  1. $0 Deterministic pedagogy lint on the Storyboard IR: states-the-definition (the Dalton lesson, generalized — the target concept’s name must appear in narration), term-introduced-before-used, one-new-idea-per-scene, narration/on-screen redundancy check. Each rule is a gate in the existing storyboard_shape_problems pattern.
  2. metered Rubric judge as advisor (never gate yet): score PARTS plans and storyboards on Mayer-derived dimensions; log scores to the sidecar; watch whether they correlate with anything (human preference, re-watch, P01’s ratings) before trusting them.
  3. research Validation: a small quiz-based comprehension study over generated videos — does any build-time score predict learning? Only a proxy that survives this earns gate status.
  4. research Closing the biggest loop: learner outcomes flowing back into planner prompts and pacing models — the educational version of the repair loop, currently ◇ everywhere.
📏 How we’d measure it

Lint-rule hit-rates on the existing storyboard cache (10 boards, measured, sit in .storyboard_cache/ ready to be linted today); advisor-score vs. human-rating correlation on the video corpus; and ultimately quiz-delta per video — acknowledging that last one is a study, not a script.
