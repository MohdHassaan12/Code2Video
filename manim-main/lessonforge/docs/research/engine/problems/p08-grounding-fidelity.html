08

Grounding without a fidelity gate

Source material flows into the storyboard prompt — and nothing checks what flows out against it. In a system whose product is teaching facts to students, an invented number is indistinguishable from a sourced one.
problems / 08
C · models own content — and content is ungated T2 applied research Maturity: L1 for fidelity (stage A graded L2 overall)
⚙️ Mechanics today
  • Grounding resolution: ce/storyboard_context.resolve_context walks YouTube transcript → content_store → none, and the chosen text rides into the storyboard prompt as context.
  • The storyboard’s admission gates are strong but orthogonal: truncation, scene shape, on-screen language (storyboard_shape_problems, normalize_onscreen_language in app/storyboard.py) — none compares output claims to input source.
  • The roadmap’s own words: “no citation of which chunk grounded which claim; nothing prevents the storyboard from inventing numbers/examples not in the source” pipeline-roadmap.md:63-64. The planned fix — a “Rule 0” prompt rule plus a fidelity spot-check with a ≥95% pilot bar — is specified and unbuilt pipeline-roadmap.md:66-69.
🕳️ The gap — why this is research-grade

The engineering half (thread chunk ids through the IR, spot-check claims) is squarely T2. The open half is a tension the field hasn’t resolved and this domain sharpens: good teaching invents. A worked example not in the textbook, a simplified number, an analogy — pedagogically excellent, factually “unsourced.” A naive faithfulness gate would reject exactly the storyboards a good teacher writes; no gate at all ships hallucinated physics to schoolchildren. The research question is a typed fidelity contract: which claim classes must be sourced (definitions, laws, constants, formulas), which may be invented (examples, analogies), and how a gate tells them apart reliably in Hinglish narration.

📎 Evidence
  • Stage A graded L2 with the fidelity gap named as its first weakness pipeline-roadmap.md:60-69.
  • The IR has no field for provenance: StoryboardScene (app/schemas.py:89) carries narration, elements, events, composition — no source_chunks. (Adding one is a schema export + --regen, drift-gated by test_ir_contracts.py — the mechanism exists.)
  • Upstream precedent for the failure class: the truncation incident shipped a video that “never states the law” pipeline-roadmap.md:708 — content defects pass silently today unless they break shape.
📚 Prior art citation-check before external use

Attributed generation and citation-faithfulness evaluation are active LLM research areas (attribution benchmarks, NLI-based faithfulness scoring, post-hoc revision pipelines in the RARR style); results are strong on extractive QA and weaker on free-form generation — and educational rewriting is maximally free-form. The claim-typing angle (must-source vs. may-invent) appears in journalism/fact-checking tooling but has no standard treatment for pedagogy.

🪜 Solution sketch — cheap → deep
  1. $0 Provenance plumbing: chunk ids on the grounding text, a source_chunks field per scene in the Storyboard IR (+ --regen), and a gate that rejects a board citing chunks that don’t exist. Deterministic, no model in the loop.
  2. $0 Claim-class lint: regex/parse-level detection of numbers, named laws and formulas in narration; any hit in a scene with no cited chunk flags the board for review — crude, zero-cost, catches the worst class (invented constants).
  3. metered The roadmap’s fidelity spot-check: sample N claims per board, verify each against its cited chunk with an NLI/LLM verifier; enforce the ≥95% pilot bar before cache-accept.
  4. research The typed contract: a claim taxonomy for teaching content, a gate that distinguishes sourced-required from invention-allowed, and measurement of the pedagogy cost of enforcement (does fidelity gating make videos worse teachers? — ties to P09).
📏 How we’d measure it

Fidelity rate (verified claims / sampled claims) per board, trending on the bulk suite — the roadmap already names the ≥95% bar; false-rejection rate on boards a subject expert rates pedagogically sound (the gate must not flunk good teaching); and zero invented constants/formulas as a hard floor.
