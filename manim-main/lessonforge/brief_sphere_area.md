# Surface Area of a Sphere

duration: 3
goal: Calculate the surface area of a sphere of radius r (4 pi r^2) using the string-winding experiment.
Class 9 Maths: Surface Areas and Volumes.

1. Take a sphere of radius 7 cm. A string is wound completely around it until its whole surface is covered.
2. Unwind the string from the sphere. On paper, draw four flat circles, each with the same radius, 7 cm.
3. The string from the sphere completely covers all four circles with none left over.
4. Each circle has area pi r^2, which is 154 cm^2. Four circles make 4 pi r^2, which is 616 cm^2. That is the surface area of the sphere.
